About the practice

Braidwood is an independent, founder-led recruitment practice based in Glasgow and working across the UK. We were founded on a straightforward observation: that the appointments which matter most — in technology, in policy and governance, in the specialist professions — are best made by small teams who know the field, keep a small book of work, and stay with every search to its end.

01What we stand for
I

Depth over volume

We would rather run a small number of searches properly than a large number approximately. It shapes everything: how many assignments we accept, how we map a field, and how we report on it.

II

Honesty in both directions

Clients hear what the market actually looks like, not what keeps a mandate alive. Candidates hear a straight answer about fit, timing and process — including when the answer is no.

III

Patience as method

The right person for a specialist role is often not looking. Part of the work is knowing the field well enough to find them, and part is having earned the standing to approach them properly.

02How an engagement runs
  • The first conversationWe start by understanding the role properly — its context, its constraints, and what good looks like — before anything is searched, promised or sent.
  • The search, done openlyClients receive a mapped, honest view of the field: who is plausible, who is reachable, and what the market actually looks like for the brief as written.
  • Shortlists with substanceFew candidates, well known — each one spoken with, each worth the client's time, each with a straight account of strengths and gaps.
  • Through to settledOffer conversations handled carefully, and contact kept through notice periods, start dates and the first months in post.
03Discretion

Recruitment is handled in confidence as a matter of course: candidate details move nowhere without a conversation first, and client circumstances are discussed with no one. It is a small profession; behaving well in it is both principle and strategy.

04The founder

The practice was founded by Iain Caldwell, an independent recruiter who works across technology and policy appointments with a particular interest in emerging roles and new professions. Iain works directly with every client and candidate, keeps a small book of assignments so each one gets proper attention, and sees each through personally.

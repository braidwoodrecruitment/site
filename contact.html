We are based in Glasgow and work across the UK. Replies come within a few working days — sooner where a role or a move is time-sensitive.

If you are hiring

A short note about the role, the organisation and its context is the best start. If it falls within our areas — technology, risk & regulated markets, professional services, or the emerging-roles desk — we will take it from there with a proper conversation about the brief and the market before anything else happens.

If you are considering a move

A few lines about your work and the direction you are weighing are enough. We keep candidate relationships deliberately unhurried: whether the timing is now or eventually, it helps to be known before the right role appears — and it costs nothing to be.

Braidwood Recruitment is an independent practice. Every enquiry is read and answered by the founder — there is no intake team, and nothing is signed up to on your behalf without a conversation first.
